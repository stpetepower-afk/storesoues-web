# Production Brief — Hallelujah ONE™ Concept Vision

**Purpose:** Vision casting / fundraising / stakeholder alignment
**Status:** Conceptual — *not* permit drawings
**Timeline:** 7-day rapid visualization sprint
**Site:** 3548 5th Ave S, St. Petersburg, FL

This brief is a production checklist. Hand it to whoever runs Meshy and D5 Render and
they can execute Phases 1–3 without further direction.

---

## Phase 0 — Define the story (today)

The emotional narrative comes first, before a single asset is generated.

**The vision, in one line:**
> A four-unit sustainable workforce housing community integrated with mobility, food,
> technology, and human empowerment.

**The feeling:** dignity · belonging · sustainability · safety · opportunity · community.

**Not:** apartments · construction · real estate.

---

## Phase 1 — Generate the concept model (Days 1–2)

**Tool:** Meshy — first-pass massing model.

### Master prompt

```
Modern sustainable four-unit workforce housing community in St. Petersburg, Florida.
Two-story contemporary fourplex designed for economic mobility. Each residence has
private entrances, balconies, large energy-efficient windows, natural wood accents,
tropical landscaping, native Florida plants, community garden beds, solar panel roof
system, green roof elements, rainwater collection features, accessible pathways,
bicycle storage, EV charging stations, and a welcoming pedestrian courtyard.
Architecture inspired by coastal Florida modern design with luxury affordable housing
principles. Include community gathering space, shaded outdoor seating, fruit trees,
native landscaping, and smart home technology elements. Photorealistic architectural
visualization style.
```

### Required exports

| Format | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **GLB** | Web / iPad viewing |
| **FBX** | Render engines (D5) |
| **OBJ** | Backup |

Drop these into `01_MASTER_RENDERING/` (source model) once generated.

## Phase 2 — Build the experience world (Days 3–5)

**Tool:** D5 Render — import the model and add the emotional layer: morning sunlight,
families walking, gardens moving in wind, vehicles arriving, community life. The model
becomes a *story*.

### Scene construction — four layers

**Housing layer**
- 4 residences
- premium-but-attainable interiors
- natural materials
- smart home technology

**Sustainability layer**
- solar roof
- native landscaping
- rain gardens
- compost area
- community garden

**Mobility layer**
- EV charging
- future autonomous-vehicle zone
- community transportation access

**Human layer** *(the most important — the project is about people, not buildings)*
- veterans
- families
- children
- seniors
- workforce residents
