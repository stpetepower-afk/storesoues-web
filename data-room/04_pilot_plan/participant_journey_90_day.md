# 90-Day Participant Journey — Pinellas Pilot (Phase 0)

Structured measurement cycle for each enrolled participant. Every milestone is
recorded against a baseline so gains are verifiable, not asserted.

## Enrollment (Day 0)
- Intake and **baseline capture**: housing status, employment, hourly wage,
  monthly income, savings, transportation access, community support.
- Baseline written to `participant_baselines` (see the reality-data schema).

## Day 30 — Stabilization
- Housing stabilization confirmed through Mac House transitional track.
- Career assessment + pathway mapping via the **AI Career Launch Mentor**.
- (Planned) Self-sovereign DID wallet provisioning — Web3 layer, not yet built.

## Day 60 — Capability
- Active credential track underway (e.g., TWIC, CDL, or logistics).
- Financial savings routine initialized.
- Interim measurement recorded to `participant_outcomes`.

## Day 90 — Placement
- Enterprise placement in a partner network (apparel logistics or regional
  medical courier), with **verified income**.
- Full outcome measurement + Economic Mobility Index (EMI) computed.
- Human case-manager review of the cycle; learnings captured to the improvement
  log.

## Measurement integrity
- Every data point is de-identified (initials / ID only — no full personal data
  in the repo).
- No outcome is reported to a funder until it is measured and human-reviewed.
- Targets: placement 75%+, 90-day retention 85%+ — reported as *current vs.
  target* until real results exist.
