# Funding & Grant Strategy — HUD CoC + USDA REAP

Phased approach to non-dilutive capital. Amounts and dates should be confirmed
with each funder before use in a submission.

| Opportunity | Range | Target date | First action |
|---|---|---|---|
| HUD CoC FY2026 (FL-502 / Pinellas) | $500K+ | ~Aug 27, 2026 | Engage the Homeless Leadership Alliance for FY2026 pathway guidance |
| USDA REAP Q4 | $250K+ | ~Nov 1, 2026 | Coordinate rural/agricultural (H3O) development scaling |

## Positioning (the CoC move)

Lead with **alignment to HUD system performance measures** — length of time
homeless, returns to homelessness, income growth at exit — not with a dollar
ask. Ask the Continuum of Care lead for **guidance on the correct FY2026
pathway**; the funding conversation follows the alignment conversation.

## The five-stage capital ladder

1. **Now:** local municipal alignment + pilot funding (HUD CoC Pinellas / FL-502).
2. Regional foundation partnerships + CDFI loan facilities.
3. State and federal government procurement contracts.
4. Enterprise B2B logistics + corporate ESG sponsorships.
5. Self-sustaining operating model fueled by enterprise revenue.

## Honest note

The local pools referenced in planning (Penny IV, CDBG-DR, CRA TIF) are
county/city-wide totals, **not** the ministry's request amount. Do not present
a pool size as an award or an ask.
