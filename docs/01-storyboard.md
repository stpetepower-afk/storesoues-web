# 01 — 3D Experience Storyboard

*St. Pete to Mac House & Beyond.* The narrative spine for the Digital Twin
walkthrough — the shot list a 3D/video team (Spline, Blender, Unreal, or a drone
edit) builds against. Aesthetic: **Elevated Clarity** (warm, human-centered).

## Opening

> "Welcome to Hallelujah ONE™ — The Human Empowerment Operating System."

## Scene 1 — The corridor (aerial)

Camera flies over, in order:

1. St. Petersburg skyline
2. South St. Pete corridor
3. 3548 5th Avenue South (the site)
4. Bicycle trails and pedestrian paths
5. PSTA bus connection
6. Downtown St. Pete
7. USF St. Petersburg
8. BayCare health system
9. VA corridor
10. Gas Plant District redevelopment

## Scene 2 — Arrival at the campus

- **Exterior:** historic Mac House identity, landscaping, community garden, walking paths
- Bicycle/e-bike station, EV charging area, transportation hub
- **Backyard Wellness Oasis:** infrared therapy, sauna, meditation space, outdoor classrooms

## Scene 3 — Mobility Division

- EV fleet (Tesla Model 3 + BrightDrop 600)
- NEMT operations
- PSTA connection
- Future autonomous integration

## Scene 4 — Career Launch Engine

- AI training room, career counseling, resume lab, digital literacy stations
- Logistics, Technology, and Enterprise tracks

## Scene 5 — H3O™ Social Enterprise

- Commercial kitchen, production line, farmers-market preparation

## Closing message

> "Recovery is not only housing. Recovery is rebuilding the whole person."

## Production notes

- **Web-ready target:** an interactive Spline scene or Webflow embed, linked from
  the handoff landing page as "Digital Twin."
- Keep transitions smooth and cinematic; warm sunlight; real spaces over stock.
- Each scene maps to a dashboard pillar, so the walkthrough and the live
  Command Center tell the same story.
