Wire it to live sources

  • Revenue — connect the Stripe MCP; ask Claude to pull the last 7 days of charges by product.
  • Downloads — App Store Connect / Play Console export (or their API).
  • Ad spend & ROAS — the Meta Ads MCP for spend; ROAS = revenue ÷ spend, computed here.
  • Posting — a Buffer-style MCP to publish one command to several platforms.
  • Support — your help-desk export for ticket counts; an agent skill can draft replies from your knowledge base.
  • Daily briefing — once sources are connected, this can run as a scheduled Routine that refreshes these numbers before you sit down.
Honest note: connectors are added in claude.ai → Settings → Connectors and each needs its own account (Stripe, Meta, Buffer). This page can't add them for you — but once they're connected, the numbers flow in and the briefing can be automated. No voice assistant or desktop-control layer is involved; this is a dashboard plus scheduled refresh.
