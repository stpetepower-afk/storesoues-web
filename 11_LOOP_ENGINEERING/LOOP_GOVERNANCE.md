# Loop Governance — Loop Engineering

How loops are governed, and how the Web3 trust layer fits (as a future design).

## Governance rules

- Every new module includes a Loop Engineering section answering the Loop Check.
- No metric is shown externally unless it is measured or clearly labeled as a
  target/placeholder.
- Automations that *act* (not just recommend) require human sign-off until the
  loop has a proven measurement history.

## Web3 trust loop (design only — not built)

A future layer for **verifiable** credentials and impact records:

- **Layer 1 — Private AI memory:** internal operations and learning.
- **Layer 2 — Cloud database:** operational records and analytics (AWS).
- **Layer 3 — Blockchain proof layer:** verified milestones and credentials.
- **Layer 4 — Community governance:** decision evolution.

Positioning: **Web3 is future privacy/credential infrastructure, not the
headline.** The headline stays housing stability + workforce mobility +
economic independence. Nothing blockchain is implemented yet; this file records
the intended design so it isn't mistaken for a running system.
